# Witch Brew — 9oz Amber | Smoke, Cardamom & Labdanum

## Highlights
- Clean burn, phthalate-free soy blend
- Hand-poured small batch in the USA
- Long, even burn ~45+ hours
- Moody, atmospheric throw

## Description
Incense without the ash. A velvet curl of smoke, toasted cardamom, and resinous labdanum. Hypnotic, night-market energy—perfect for autumn, alt-decor, and ritual baths.

## Materials
Soy blend wax, cotton wick, premium fragrance oils (phthalate-free), amber glass, metal lid.

## Size / Burn
9oz | ~45+ hours | Ideal for dens, studios, living rooms.

## Care
Trim wick to 1/4" before each burn. First burn 2–3 hrs until the melt pool reaches the edge.
